# Story E63S08: End-to-end live-worker verification + Epic closure

## User Story

**As a** tournament organizer,
**I want** proof that a large-phase optimization on the dispatcher really produces an
optimized schedule end-to-end,
**so that** "Leg 2 works" is a demonstrated fact — and Epic E63 closes on evidence.

---

## Context

E63S01–S07 complete the Tournament Manager side of the SlotOpt-Dispatcher integration: the
Leg-2 result round-trip, the shared worker compute path, the embedded worker (core,
host-protection, controls), the Leg-2 operator-cancel, and the dispatcher offline-status UI.
Each story verifies its own slice. This closing story verifies the **whole TM↔dispatcher
integration** end-to-end with a live worker, and closes the epic.

The human-chosen verification bar is "end-to-end with a live worker": a Leg-2 job submitted
by TM, solved by a real worker, finalized by the dispatcher, with TM fetching and applying
the optimum — proven by a cross-process integration test (the E41S06 cross-process IT
pattern is the precedent).

Closure also corrects the operator documentation (`slot-optimization-routing.md` still
describes Leg 2 and admin-cancel as they were *before* this epic) and propagates the E63
governance artefacts.

---

## Acceptance Criteria

### Testing

- [ ] **AC-TEST-E2E-LEG2-LIVE-WORKER** — a cross-process integration test demonstrates the
  full Leg-2 round trip: TM submits a large-phase (N > threshold) slot-optimization job to a
  running `vvwt-slotopt-dispatcher`; a live `vvwt-slotopt-standalone-worker` solves the
  packets; the dispatcher finalizes; TM fetches and applies the optimum; the phase's matches
  reflect the optimized schedule. The harness mechanism is Delivery's choice (per the
  E41S06 cross-process IT precedent) — the AC is the behavioural round trip.
- [ ] **AC-TEST-E2E-EMBEDDED-WORKER-CONTRIBUTES** — the end-to-end verification also
  exercises the TM embedded worker (enabled) contributing packet compute: a job is solved
  with TM's own embedded worker participating in the pool.
- [ ] **AC-TEST-E2E-LEG3-FALLBACK** — the end-to-end verification covers the degraded path:
  with the dispatcher unreachable, a large-phase optimization still falls through to Leg 3
  and yields an optimized phase (the offline-operability guarantee, DEC-15 — never
  regressed). The Leg-2-wire-error→Leg-3 fall-through and the Leg-2-cancel→Best-So-Far
  graceful paths (Q-2) are verified by E63S02 and E63S06 respectively; E63S08's end-to-end
  scope is the Leg-2 success round trip plus this offline fallback.
- [ ] **AC-TEST-REACTOR-SUITE-GREEN** — `mvn verify` is BUILD SUCCESS for `vvwt-tm-web` and
  the reactor build is GREEN (DEC-54); no pre-existing test regressed.

### Governance

- [ ] **AC-GOV-OPERATOR-DOC-CORRECTED** — `vvwt-tm-web/docs/slot-optimization-routing.md` is
  corrected, against a fresh read of the doc at HEAD, so it matches the post-E63 reality:
  Leg 2 actually fetches and applies the dispatcher's computed optimum; operator admin-cancel
