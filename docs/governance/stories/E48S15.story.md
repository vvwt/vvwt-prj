# Story E48S15: UX-Fix — parentRouteMap Sub-Route Targets (P→Tournaments)

## User Story

**As a** Tournament Organizer,
**I want** the back-arrow on Tournament-sub-routes (Teams, Draft, Audio, TimerLink, Photos, CertificateTemplate, SlotOptimization) to return me to the Tournament-Liste,
**so that** I do not get stranded on the rarely-used Tournament-Edit form (which I typically only fill in once during initial setup) and need to navigate further to reach the list I came from.

---

## Context

Per Brief D-2 / D-5 / O-9 (`discovery-2026-05-06-tournament-form-bugs`): `vvwt-tm-web/src/main/ui/src/lib/parentRouteMap.ts:26-42` declaratively maps every Tournament-sub-route to its semantic parent. E47S01/S02 set 7 sub-routes to point at `/tournaments/:tournamentId/edit` on the rationale that the Tournament-Edit form is the resource-tree parent of those sub-routes.

User feedback 2026-05-06: "Es gibt in der Turnier-Bearbeitung keinen Direkten Weg zur Team-Bearbeitung! [...] Tournament-Edit-Form ist One-Time-Setup; Organisator muss diese Seite nur ganz zu Beginn ausfüllen und fertig. Es gibt keinen Grund, dass er immer wieder in die Bearbeitungsseite geleitet wird." — the resource-tree-parent semantics conflict with the user's mental model "back = where I came from" and force a 2-step navigation back to the list (`/tournaments → /:id/edit → /:id/teams → back-arrow → /:id/edit → back-arrow → /tournaments`).

Per Brief D-2 user-decision (P→Tournaments): keep the declarative architecture (no browser-history fallback per S-3), but redirect the 7 sub-routes' targets directly to `/tournaments`. Per Brief O-9: this VEREINHEITLICHT the existing pattern — `/tournaments/:tournamentId/phases` (added by E48S05) already maps to `/tournaments` (skipping `/edit`); the 7 sub-routes inherited from E47S01 are the inconsistent residue. The fix removes the inconsistency rather than introducing a new pattern.
