# Story E37S11: Atomic cleanup — rm -rf vvwt-dispatcher/ + verify no remaining artifactId/FQN references + final mvn verify GREEN

## User Story

**As a** maintainer of the slot-optimization service after E37 reconstruction is complete,
**I want** the legacy `vvwt-dispatcher/` directory deleted from the filesystem, all remaining `vvwt-dispatcher` artifactId and `de.vvwt.dispatcher.*` FQN references verified absent across the entire reactor, AND a final clean reactor `mvn verify`,
**so that** the legacy module leaves zero residual surface and Epic E37 closes with a fully-modernized slot-optimization dispatcher under the new `vvwt-slotopt-*` naming convention.

---

## Context

Per Brief D-7 + S-7 + T-8: legacy `vvwt-dispatcher/` was excluded from the parent POM at E37S01 (Coexistence Option δ-c — Maven-module-level mechanical isolation). The directory has remained physically present on filesystem, untouched, throughout E37S02–S10 to reinforce the no-inspection discipline (Brief C-16). This is the final story that performs the atomic deletion.

The story is intentionally compact: deletion + verification + governance propagation.
