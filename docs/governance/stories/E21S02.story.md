# Story E21S02: Tournament Aggregate (root) — reconstruction-in-place with TDD

## User Story

**As a** maintainer of the `tournament` bounded context,
**I want** the root `Tournament` aggregate, its service, its two repositories (tenant-scoped + CrudRepository), its REST controller, and its three request/response DTOs reconstructed under `de.vvwt.tm.tournament.*` with a failing test preceding every implementation class,
**so that** the hub aggregate of the TM domain exists cleanly on the new Modulith boundary, all six downstream Track-3 contexts (`scoring`, `certificate`, `print`, `display`, `timer`, `slotopt-integration`) can target `de.vvwt.tm.tournament::api` from day one, and the DAO-test schema helper (`TenantDaoTestSupport.applyTournamentSchema()`) exists for every subsequent tournament DAO story.

---

## Context

Epic E21 reconstructs the `tournament` bounded context under DEC-21 (Spring Modulith) + DEC-22 (TDD reconstruction-in-place). `tournament` is the TM hub: teams, matches, phases, and the `Tournament` root aggregate itself. Per Session Brief `discovery-2026-04-20-e21-full-refinement` D-10 (Approach C, hub-first), reconstructing `tournament` is the **first** Track-3 move and establishes `de.vvwt.tm.tournament::api` before any downstream context retargets.

This story — the FIRST reconstruction story in E21 — handles the root aggregate and the CRUD controller that sits on top of it. Per Brief D-4 (aggregate-oriented DDD decomposition), `Tournament` is the natural first cut: a root aggregate with its service, repos, controller, and DTOs. Legacy FQNs: `de.vvwt.tm.domain.Tournament` (inventory line 190, 15-importer boundary-API root), `de.vvwt.tm.domain.TournamentService` (line 191), `de.vvwt.tm.domain.repo.TournamentRepository` (line 311, 15 importers — highest-imported repo in tournament context, strong boundary-API), `de.vvwt.tm.domain.repo.TournamentCrudRepository` (line 310), `de.vvwt.tm.infrastructure.web.TournamentController` (line 460), and the three DTOs at `de.vvwt.tm.infrastructure.web.dto.Tournament*Request/Response` (lines 446–448).

**TournamentRulesController is NOT in S02 scope** — it exposes `MatchFormat` (reconstructed in S05) and scoring/validation rule registries (scoring-context legacy, reconstructed in E22). Reconstructing `TournamentRulesController` in S02 would force a transitional legacy import from new code into legacy `de.vvwt.tm.domain.MatchFormat` etc., violating AC-NO-LEGACY-TOUCH spirit. Per Review-Cycle-2 Q-1(a) human decision (2026-04-20), `TournamentRulesController` moves to **E21S10** (Cross-cutting Web), where MatchGeneratorRegistry from S08 is available and legacy scoring-rule-registry imports are mechanically rewired at S13 cutover per DEC-32.

Per DEC-21 end-state and Brief D-8: `Tournament`, `TournamentRepository`, `TournamentController` land at `de.vvwt.tm.tournament.*` (public API surface of the Modulith module). `TournamentService`, `TournamentCrudRepository`, and the three DTOs land at `de.vvwt.tm.tournament.internal.*` (implementation) — specifically the DTOs at `de.vvwt.tm.tournament.internal.dto.*`. Controllers are part of the module's public surface per conventions.md (e): "REST controllers are authored at their DEC-21 target package `de.vvwt.tm.{context}` root."

Per DEC-22, each class is written test-first — a failing test precedes every implementation commit. No characterization tests against the legacy classes. The legacy `de.vvwt.tm.domain.*` code remains untouched until the E21S13 atomic cutover commit (DEC-13 + Brief D-6/DEC-32). Consumer-context code (e.g., `infrastructure.score.*`, `infrastructure.display.*`, `slotopt.*`) is also untouched in this story — mechanical rewire is S13-scope.

REST tests follow C-13 Hybrid-Split-Minimalist-IT methodology (E20S02 canon, conventions.md §REST Controller Integration Tests): per controller, one `@WebMvcTest` slice + one `@SpringBootTest(webEnvironment=RANDOM_PORT)` IT. DAO tests follow DEC-26 three rules. Per Session Brief C-15, this story also delivers `TenantDaoTestSupport.applyTournamentSchema()` — the helper that subsequent tournament DAO stories (S03, S04, S05) reuse to load the root-migration subset (V1+V2+V3+V4+V5+V7+V8+V9+V10+V11+V12+V14+V16, plus auth/V1) into a test DataSource in version order.
