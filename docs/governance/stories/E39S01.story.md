# Story: Operationalize DEC-44 D1+D2 — WebModuleTestConfig `@Primary` (3/4) + 10 `web/*ControllerIT.java` annotation swap + `patterns/conventions.md` § REST Controller IT update + `mvn verify` GREEN

## User Story

**As a** Tournament Manager Delivery Agent operating under DEC-44+DEC-45 governance,
**I want** the existing 10 `web/*ControllerIT.java` files migrated from `@ApplicationModuleTest(ALL_DEPENDENCIES, RANDOM_PORT)` to `@SpringBootTest(RANDOM_PORT, classes = TournamentManagerApplication.class)` and `WebModuleTestConfig` refactored to add `@Primary` on 3 of 4 auth-substitute beans,
**so that** the `de.vvwt.tm.web` module's IT canon aligns with DEC-44 D1 (Clause E §Sub-Clause-3 retro-correction), future Wave-2 epics author new web-module ITs directly under the corrected canon, and the governance-trail-gap inherited from E24S06 is closed.

---

## Context

DEC-40 Clause E §Sub-Clause-3 (2026-04-22) introduced a structural-escape SHALL trigger: web-module ITs must switch to `@SpringBootTest(RANDOM_PORT)` once `web.allowedDependencies` covers ≥6 entries (`tenant + 5 bounded contexts`). The threshold was met at E24S06 (which added `print` — the 5th bounded context excluding tenant); no DEC-amendment was authored at the time, leaving the structural-escape SHALL operationally unfulfilled.

DEC-44 (authored in the same Discovery session as this story) RETRO-CORRECTS the missed activation. DEC-45 (companion DEC) records the L2-stays verdict for the Wave-2 trajectory.

This story operationalizes DEC-44 D1 (annotation swap) + DEC-44 D2 (`WebModuleTestConfig` refactor) + DEC-44 §Impact `patterns/conventions.md` update + DEC-31 propagate-governance two-commit contract. DEC-44 + DEC-45 + DEC-38 amendment-pointer + DEC-40 amendment-pointer + `index.md` Decision-Registry rows + `_log.md` increment are NOT in scope of this story — those are part of the Discovery commit that authors this story (per DEC-34/-36/-41 git-history precedent).
