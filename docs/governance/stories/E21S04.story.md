# Story E21S04: Team + TeamAvatar + TeamAvatarRating Aggregates — reconstruction-in-place with TDD

## User Story

**As a** maintainer of the `tournament` bounded context,
**I want** the `Team`, `TeamAvatar`, and `TeamAvatarRating` entities + their six repositories + `TeamService` + `TeamController` + the five Team DTOs reconstructed under `de.vvwt.tm.tournament.*` / `.tournament.internal.*` with a failing test preceding every implementation,
**so that** team management and slot-assignment aggregates sit cleanly on the new Modulith boundary and the slotopt, display, print, and scoring contexts can target the new `Team*` public-API repositories without retargeting later.

---

## Context

Epic E21 reconstructs the `tournament` hub before any other Track-3 context (Brief D-10 Approach C). After E21S02 (Tournament root + schema helper) and alongside E21S03 (Phase cluster), this story delivers the **team cluster**: `Team` (the tournament team), `TeamAvatar` (the assignment of a team to a phase slot — the central slotopt consumer), and `TeamAvatarRating` (aggregate standings per TeamAvatar per phase, consumed by display and scoring).

Per Session Brief D-4 (aggregate-oriented DDD decomposition), team + avatar + rating cluster naturally. Per D-8 and inventory: `Team` (inventory line 185 — boundary-API, 10 importers via `print/LaufzettelAssembler`), `TeamAvatar` (line 186 — boundary-API, 12 importers via `slotopt/PhaseToRawPhaseDefMapper`, highest-imported assignment type), `TeamAvatarRating` (line 187 — boundary-API, 4 importers via `display/DisplayOverviewService`). Repositories: `TeamRepository` (line 307, 8 importers — boundary-API), `TeamAvatarRepository` (line 305 — boundary-API, 9 importers — slotopt), `TeamAvatarRatingRepository` (line 304 — boundary-API candidate; elevated during refinement because the entity is boundary-API). CrudRepository twins + `TeamService` + the five DTOs + `TeamController` round out the surface.

Per Brief D-8 and conventions.md (e), `TeamController` lands at `de.vvwt.tm.tournament.TeamController` (public API per DEC-21). REST tests follow C-13 Hybrid-Split-Minimalist-IT. Per DEC-22, RED-first TDD per class. Per DEC-26, DAO tests reuse the `TenantDaoTestSupport.applyTournamentSchema()` helper delivered by E21S02 — this story does NOT extend the helper.

Legacy `de.vvwt.tm.domain.*` classes and all consumer-context code (`infrastructure.print.*`, `slotopt.*`, `infrastructure.display.*`, etc.) remain untouched until E21S13 atomic cutover per DEC-32.
